Karaoke 101

New to karaoke night? Here’s how to have the best time.

  1. Pick a song you know by heart.
  2. Aim to be loud, not perfect.
  3. Clap for everyone, even the off-key ones.
  4. Shout “Dzô!” between songs.
  5. Sip a sweet iced coffee to keep your voice going.

Frequently Asked Questions:

What time do you open?

We open at 4 PM and stay open late. For tonight’s exact closing time, check Google Maps or give us a call at (980) 237-9888.

Can I order takeout?

Yes! Call us at (980) 237-9888, tell us what you’d like, and we’ll let you know when it’s ready for pickup.

Do you really have karaoke?

We do. Saigon Night is a Vietnamese kitchen and a karaoke spot, so come hungry and bring your singing voice. Follow our Facebook page for karaoke nights and weekend specials.

Can I bring a big group or plan a party?

Absolutely. Use the form on our Contact page or call ahead with your party size and date, and we’ll help you plan a night out.

Where are you located?

5917 Albemarle Rd, Charlotte, NC 28212. Get directions.

What should I order if it’s my first time?

Ask us what’s cooking tonight, or browse the full menu. A steaming bowl of noodle soup and a strong, sweet Vietnamese iced coffee is a classic start.

Do you have vegetarian options or allergy-friendly dishes?

Please call us before you visit and tell us about any dietary needs or allergies. We’ll let you know what we can do.

How do I say “cheers” in Vietnamese?

Dzô! (say “zoh”). Shout it with a glass in hand, and you’re ready for karaoke night.

Why are there lanterns on your website?

Because Saigon never sleeps, and neither do we. Try bumping them with your cursor: the harder you hit, the further they swing.
